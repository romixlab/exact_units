The digits are exact where the steps are a fraction of integers with a finite decimal (powers of ten, `Pow2` scales, Q15, Q31, binary angles; before, binary scales went through `f64`), and a precision rounds that exact value to the nearest, a tie to even, as Rust's float formatting does (`Rounding::NearestEven`). Width, fill, alignment, `+` and `0` apply to the whole field with the unit (`{:#10.2}` of 1500 mV is `    1.50 V`). The same for `Complex` (`{:#.3}` is `0.500-0.250j`, the precision per part), `Polar` and a `Point`'s `Display` (`{:.1}` is `25.6 °C`). Examples `07_tone_generator` and `14_iq_downconvert` print this way and lose their `as f32 / 32768.0` helpers.
* Decimal literals as constants (NUM-7): `fixed!(0.1)` is the `Fixed` its context names, holding the stored value nearest to the literal in units of its unit, worked out at compile time: `fixed!(0.1)` in Q30 is `107_374_182`, `fixed!(2.5e-3)` in `Micro` is 2500. Integer storages round the exact fraction to nearest, ties to even; float storages take the nearest float. Any scale that is a fraction of integers (`Pow2`, `Pow10`, `Rational`, products); a scale with π in it, a value that doesn't fit the storage or its bounds, or a negative value of an unsigned storage doesn't compile. `fixed!(0.5, -0.25)` is a `Complex`. `Fixed::from_decimal(Decimal)` is the same as a `const fn`. `macro_rules` and `const fn` only, no proc macro.

### ⚠️ Breaking

- `Fraction` is `±num/den · 10^exp` (NUM-33, P2401#10nov-1910b): a power of ten apart from its `u128` mantissa, so atomic-scale values are exact. `numer()` and `denom()` return `Option<u128>`, the plain lowest terms, `None` past `u128`; `mantissa()` and `exp10()` give the parts. `Fraction::pow10` takes an `i32` and is infallible; `pow2` takes an `i32`. The form is canonical (the exponent is the value's power of 5, clamped into the range where the mantissa fits), so `0.2` is `2·10⁻¹` and `24 MHz` is `24·10⁶` Hz inside. Serde's binary form of a `Fraction` and an `Exact` is the quadruple `(negative, numerator, denominator, exponent)`, checked canonical on reading. The wire bytes of a `Fraction` (feature `shrink_wrap`, a `CurveForm`'s parameters) gain the `i32` exponent, and a read refuses a form that isn't canonical. `Fraction::from_f64` now gives every float of magnitude 2⁻¹⁰⁷ or more and below 2¹⁸³ (smaller ones with fewer significant bits down to 2⁻¹⁸²), not just those within `u128`; subnormals and floats from 2¹⁸³ up are `None`. The wider arithmetic costs flash: one PQ encode on a Cortex-M4F (`tests/curve_size.rs`) takes 62 920 B by the float-free edge and 59 900 B by the `f64` one, where they took 49 300 B and 47 290 B. The float-free curve evaluation refuses a light or a parameter whose plain terms pass `u128` (`None`, `EncodeError::NotExact`); `Enclosure::compare` orders such a value exactly.
- `Fixed::try_new`, `Fixed::to_bounds` and `Point::try_from_raw` refuse with `OutOfRange<U, P, T, B>` instead of `OutOfBounds` (P2401#4nov-0554). The error holds the value refused (`value()`, and `min()`/`max()` give `B`'s ends); the unit, scale, storage and bounds are its type's, so it is as large as `T`, `no_std` and no alloc. `Display` names them all, `0 is out of 1..=63 (Ratio, u8)`, `6000 ms is out of 1..=5000 ms (Second, u16)`: the text serde already gave, which serde now gets from the same type. `Debug` is `OutOfRange(0, 1..=63)`, `defmt` prints the value and range. `?` turns it into an `OutOfBounds`, which every other refusal keeps (`Index`, `Niched`, `Complex`, `Z0`, `Coded`, `TryFrom<Duration>`). A pattern `Err(OutOfBounds)` on `try_new` becomes `Err(_)` or `Err(e)`. The wire read still refuses with shrink_wrap's `SubtypeOutOfRange`, a variant without data; carrying the value there is wire_weaver's side.
- `Kind` is gone, replaced by `QuantityExpr` (UNIT-8): `Spec(QuantityRef)` for a quantity of the tree, `RatioOf(Dim)` as before, `Derived(Dim)` for what was `Plain`. `Unit::KIND` is `Unit::QUANTITY`; `Unit` gains the required `type Bare: Unit` (the unit without the quantity `Of` gave it; `unit!`, `Per`, `Times` and `Sqrt` fill it, a hand-written impl writes `type Bare = Self;`). `UnitDef::quantity` is `Option<QuantityRef>` (on the wire the quantity's `MarkerId`), `UnitDef::kind()` is `quantity_expr()`, `ParsedUnit::kind()` is `quantity()` and `Registry::parse_unit_of` takes a `QuantityExpr`. `kind "resistance"` on a `unit!` line is `quantity Resistance`. `LevelForm` moved to `quantity`, unchanged. `Fixed::convert` and its kin, `fixed!(x in S)` and `Exact`'s conversions now convert up the tree as well as within one quantity.
- `units::Phase<A>` is `Of<PhaseAngle, A>`; `units::EnergyDensity`, `Entropy`, `DampingRatio` and `QualityFactor` are `Of` aliases over `Per<Joule, Volume>`, `HeatCapacity` and `Ratio`, no longer named units (they leave `units::ALL`, and text reads J/K and J/m³ as the plain units). The feature `levels` enables `dimensionless`.
- Hz, Bq and Bd no longer convert to one another (Frequency, Activity, SymbolRate), nor Gy and Sv (AbsorbedDose, DoseEquivalent, apart by a weighting factor): `as_quantity` is the step. A derived s⁻¹ (`Per<Ratio, Second>`) still converts to Hz, the open root.
- Text into a unit of a quantity: a named unit of a quantity of its own reads only into that one (`2 Hz` isn't a `Becquerel`), a unit that names only its dimension reads into any quantity of it, the target's (`2 V/A` into an `Ohm`, which was refused).
- The dimension's shape (UNIT-10): `Dim` holds the exponents of SI's seven true bases in an array (`exps()`, `s()`..`cd()`), each an `Exp` in halves (V/√Hz is `kg·m²·s⁻⁵ᐟ²·A⁻¹`, -64..=63½, no root flag), and every other base in one list without a count limit: the radian, the neper and its loss, the bit and a crate's own counted things are all distinguished bases, named by their `MarkerId`. A compound unit with six or more counted bases now builds (four slots before). The bases are a tree (`Bases`: a slice in `const` data, the named units of a compound type, products, quotients, a strip, a list read from the wire) read in canonical order (crate, then name), so `==`, `Hash`, `Display` and the wire agree on one form; `Dim`'s fields are private and every `Dim` adds up by construction. `DimOwned` owns its bases (sorted, in a `Vec`) and multiplies, divides, takes powers and roots at run time. Moves: `counted` and `counted(Other)` on a `unit!` line are `base` and `base(Other)`, a line's exponents may be halves (`[s -1/2]`) and a true base named twice is a compile error; `counted!(Name)` is `base!(Name)`, a `MarkerId`; `Dim::counted` is `Dim::base(MarkerId)`; `Counted`, `MAX_COUNTED`, `with_rad`, `with_bit`, `is_canonical`, the `rad`/`bit`/`root`/`counted` fields go; `strip_radian()` and `strip_neper()` are `strip(MarkerId::RADIAN)` and `strip(MarkerId::NEPER_LOSS)` (or `NEPER`), and `true_part()` drops every distinguished base; `mul`/`div` are `checked_mul`/`checked_div` (borrowing both) and `const_mul`/`const_div`, `same` is run-time and `const_same`/`const_exp_of`/`const_is_one` answer in `const` code (`None` for bases read at run time); `Kind::same`/`mul`/`div`/`level_form` are run-time, and `Kind::const_same`/`const_mul`/`const_div`/`const_level_form` their `const` twins (`Option`, `None` for bases read at run time). `Unit::BASE` is the `BaseDef` a `base` line declares (`Radian`, `Bit`, `Neper`, `NeperLoss`, a crate's `Pixel`). `UnitTable` requires `Sync` (a `Dim` read through a table borrows it), and `SIExpr::dim` gives a `DimOwned`. The wire shape is `DimWire { exps, bases }`, each exponent an `INib32` of halves; a reader refuses another count of exponents, unsorted or repeated bases and an exponent of 0, as serde does (JSON `{"exps": [{"halves": ..}], "bases": [{"base": {..}, "exp": {..}}]}`).
- `Reference` has a new required const, `HOW: ReferenceHow`: a hand-written reference says how its zero is known. `OriginDef::reference` is now a `ReferenceDef` (its `id` is the `MarkerId` the field held before). `origins::UnixEpoch` moved from `UnitZero` to `PosixScale`: a user's origin on `UnitZero` in seconds no longer converts to it at compile time, which was wrong by the leap seconds for a GPS or TAI epoch (PT-4).
- A level comes back as a typed value, not a bare float (P2401#8nov-0733): the unit can't get lost, and a result checks against `q!`, `level.is_in(q!(6.0205... Decibel))`, instead of a hand-written tolerance. They work where the float forms did, with `std` or `libm` and any unit group: `libm` now turns on `levels`, whose markers (`Decibel`, `Neper`, `Loss`) are zero-sized and add no code to a firmware, as `std` already did through `quantities`. `from_db` and `from_np` still take the bare number a setting or a reading arrives as; `to_level` and `to_gain` stay the typed edges with a rounding.
  - `Fixed::to_db(self) -> f64` is now `-> Option<Fixed<Decibel, One, f64>>`: `None` for a gain below 0, which was NaN; a gain of 0 stays −∞ dB.
  - `Fixed::to_db_f32(self) -> f32` is now `-> Option<Fixed<Decibel, One, f32>>`.
  - `Fixed::to_np(self) -> f64` is now `-> Option<Fixed<Neper, One, f64>>`, `Fixed::to_np_f32(self) -> f32` `-> Option<Fixed<Neper, One, f32>>`.
  - `Complex::to_db(self) -> f64` is now `-> Option<Fixed<Decibel, One, f64>>`, `Complex::to_db_f32(self) -> f32` `-> Option<Fixed<Decibel, One, f32>>`: `None` only for a NaN part, z = 0 stays −∞ dB.
  - `Complex::return_loss_db(self) -> f64` and `return_loss_db_f32(self) -> f32` of a Γ are now one `Complex::return_loss(self) -> Fixed<Loss<Decibel>, One, F>`, worked out in Γ's own float `F` (`f32` in `f32`, as `return_loss_db_f32` was): a loss, positive for a passive load, +∞ when matched.
- `fixed!`, `db!` and `np!` give the exact stored value or refuse (P2401#3nov-0058b, rule 3 of [What exact means](docs/start/exact.md)): a literal that is no stored value, which they rounded to the nearest one, ties to even, without naming it, is now a compile error unless the call names a rounding. The error names the two stored values around it with their exact decimals, ready to paste: `fixed!(0.1) is not a step of its storage: the steps around it are 107374182 = 0.09999999962747097015380859375 and 107374183 = 0.100000000558793544769287109375; write one of them, or name a rounding: fixed!(0.1, Floor | Ceil | Truncate | NearestEven | NearestAway)`. A float storage names the two floats around the literal (`fixed!(0.1)` is no `f32`); a scale whose steps aren't finite decimals (1/3) names the stored values only.
  - Name a rounding as the last argument: `fixed!(0.1, NearestEven)`, `fixed!(0.5, 0.1, Floor)`, `fixed!(90 in Degree, NearestEven)`, `fixed!(359.9 km/h, NearestEven)`, `db!(-3, NearestEven)`, `db!(norm_sqr -15, Floor)`, `np!(-1, NearestEven)`: any `Rounding` by name (`Truncate`, `Floor`, `Ceil`, `NearestEven`, `NearestAway`), a type of the new `literal::mode`. To keep the old values, add `NearestEven`.
  - A factor with π or ln 10 (`in Degree`, dB in Np) and the gain of every level that isn't a whole number of decades (`np!`: every level but 0) are irrational, so those calls always need a rounding; their error names the stored values in the storage's own unit, to write as `fixed!(..)`.
  - The literal is checked against the storage and the bounds before any rounding: `fixed!(10.4, NearestEven)` into `Range<0, 10>` and a Q15 literal past the last step (0.999984741) no longer compile, where they were rounded onto the last stored value.

  - The carriers changed: `literal::Literal<N, E>` is gone; `fixed!` names `literal::Lit<L, M>`, `LitIn<L, S, M>` or `LitSymbols<L, Q, M>` of a `literal::Source` the call declares, and `db!`/`np!` name `decibel::Level<L, M>`, `NormSqrLevel<L, M>` and `NeperLevel<L, M>` in place of their const-generic forms. `literal::LiteralIn` stays for `q!`.

A curve is now data of its own, `codec::CurveDef` (which function it is, `Referred::Scene` or `Display`, the light's unit and scale, the signal's domain and `Segment`s), each segment a `CurveForm` written signal to light with exact `Fraction` parameters, exponents included: `Affine`, `Power` (`gain·max(scale·x + shift, 0)^exponent + offset`, so BT.709's and sRGB's divisor inside the power stays rational), `Exp` (base e or a rational) and `RationalPower` (PQ's form). `Curve` gains `type Light: Unit` and `const DEF: CurveDef`, its `KIND` computed from the forms. `Pq::M1` .. `Pq::C3` are `Fraction`s now, `c₁ = c₃ − c₂ + 1` checked on them at compile time, and `Pq::DEF` is PQ as data. `Fraction` goes on the wire (feature `shrink_wrap`; a read refuses terms that aren't lowest).
- `units::AbsorbedDoseRate` is renamed `units::GrayPerSecond` (UNIT-12), as its quantity is now `quantities::AbsorbedDoseRate` and a quantity can't share a unit's `MarkerId`; `units::AbsorbedDoseRate` stays as an alias of it. `SievertPerHour` measures a `DoseEquivalentRate`, so it no longer converts to Gy/s or Sv/s. `units::Sievert`'s doc says what it measures: the SI's dose equivalent H, not ICRP's equivalent dose.
- `Rounding::NearestCeil` (LVL-9, P2401#10nov-1357): to the nearest, a tie toward +∞, so 2.5 is 3 and −2.5 is −2 (ITU-R BT.709's INT, JavaScript's `Math.round`). A new variant of an exhaustive public enum, so a `match` on `Rounding` outside the crate needs an arm for it. Unlike `NearestAway` it commutes with a whole offset: rounding a video signal's `Linear` before the offset gives the code the standards give by rounding after it. For a negative chroma tie (E′ = −1/448, code 127.5) `NearestCeil` gives 128, as BT.709's INT and BT.2100's Round do, where `NearestAway` gives 127. Every rounding site takes it: integer division, the fixed-point and wide paths, floats, `Fraction::round`, `requantize`, `Table` and `OneOf` rounding, and the literals (`fixed!(x, NearestCeil)`). `tests/props` walk it as every other mode against `reference.rs`, and `tests/props/video.rs` checks every E′ in steps of 1/4096 against the standard's INT with it, exactly, with no negative tie apart. Book: [Rounding](docs/maths/rounding.md), [Video codes and transfer curves](docs/fixed_point/video.md#from-e-to-a-code).
- `Magnitude` holds primes below 2⁶⁴, not 2³² (NUM-32): CODATA 2022's proton mass in kg has the prime 33452438519, its A_r(e) is the prime 5485799090441, so a measured value with up to 19 digits needs the wider slot. `PrimePower::prime` is a `u64`, `Magnitude::exp_x2` takes a `u64`, `MagnitudeError::PrimeTooLarge` is gone, and the serde form's primes are `u64`. The cost is in memory and on the wire. In memory a `PrimePower` is 10 bytes (was 6) and a `Magnitude` 202 (was 138), a `UnitDef` 496 (was 432): a firmware that holds the whole unit catalogue for `units::by_name` (`quantities` and `customary`) grows from 109 974 to 128 914 bytes of flash on thumbv6m-none-eabi (+17 %), 109 310 to 128 190 on thumbv7em-none-eabihf; a firmware without a `&'static Magnitude` (the `tools/can_fd_size` images) doesn't change. On the wire a prime is a `UVlq64` (was `UNib32`): a degree's magnitude is 15 bytes (was 13), the pound's 18 (was 17). The run-time kernel (`Magnitude::cmp_scaled`, `Fixed::from_prefix`) stays in `u32` limbs, so no 128-bit division is linked, and refuses a magnitude with a prime of 2³² or more; the `i128` and `u128` paths (`apply_int`, `to_ratio`) take it.
- `Symbols` has a sixth field, `m_u`, and `Constants` five more required items (`M_U`, `M_E`, `M_P`, `AR_E`, `AR_P`): a set of your own lists them, `None` where it has no value. `Resolved` has a third uncertainty, `m_u_rel_u`. On the wire `Symbols` is one byte longer.
- `units::Exposure` (feature `radiation`) is now `units::CoulombPerKilogram`, with `quantity crate::quantities::Exposure` (a new kind of its own, apart from a specific charge); `Exposure` stays as a type alias, as `units::AbsorbedDoseRate` is `GrayPerSecond`'s, so most code is unaffected. What changes is the marker's own identity: `<units::Exposure as Unit>::EXPR.def().unwrap().id.name` is now `"CoulombPerKilogram"`, not `"Exposure"` (UNIT-14, P2401#10nov-1911d).
- `CodecShape` has a new variant, `MantissaExponent` (LVL-13, P2401#10nov-2121b): a `match` on `CodecShape` outside the crate needs an arm for it.
- `constants::Z0` is `constants::ETA0` (CPX-7, P2401#11nov-1351): the impedance of free space μ₀c, η₀, under a name of its own. `exact_units::Z0` is a line's reference impedance (50 Ω), a different quantity, and one name for both read two ways (rule 5 of "What exact means"). Replace `constants::Z0` with `constants::ETA0`; the value is the same expression.
